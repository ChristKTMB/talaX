# Terms of use — Tala X

Version of 2026-11-05.

Tala X (talax.online) is a free web service, without accounts, that analyses
an image to say what can honestly be said about it: whether it is a camera
original or a copy reprocessed by a platform, and what its metadata says. It
is published by Christ KATUMBA KATUMBA, Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of the
Congo (<contact@talax.online>).

By using the service, you accept these terms.

## 1. What Tala X says, and what it does not

- **Tala X certifies nothing.** It never says that an image is true,
  authentic or fake. "Camera original" means the file carries the marks of a
  camera, not that what it shows is true.
- Every result is an **indication with its explanation**, to be read with
  your own judgement. Metadata can be forged by someone who knows how.
- **You alone are responsible** for what you decide to publish, or not,
  based on an analysis. You may not present a Tala X result as a
